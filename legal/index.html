Privacy

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and it does not ask for or store any personal information.

Videos. Video players are not loaded until you press play. When you do, the video is loaded from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com), and YouTube's own privacy policy then applies to that player. Links to YouTube open YouTube's site.

Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web server, may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages securely. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.
