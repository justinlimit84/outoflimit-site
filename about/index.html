About

Forgotten foundations, shown from first principles

Your phone, your bank, your password and your GPS all run on ideas most people never see — many of them decades old, buried under layers of newer technology. outoflimit digs them up.

Every story starts from something you use every day and follows it back to the idea underneath: who had it, what problem they were solving, and how it grew into the tech in your pocket. Then it shows you where that idea hits its limits, breaks, or quietly does something you didn't expect.

We don't try to tell you everything. The goal is the spark: enough understanding that you want to explore the rest yourself. That's why every story page ends with names, papers and questions to follow up.

How we work

Sources before scripts

Every date, number and name in a video is checked against primary sources first — official incident reports, research papers, standards, court records — and listed on the story's page. If only one weak source exists, we soften the claim or cut it.

Animated in code

Every frame is drawn programmatically with ManimGL, the open-source animation engine created by Grant Sanderson. No stock footage: each object on screen is built to show one idea.

One idea, no padding

Each long video covers a single idea in 8–15 minutes. If a story is complete at six minutes, it stays six minutes.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we want to know. Leave a comment on the video with your source, and we'll correct the story page and pin the correction.
